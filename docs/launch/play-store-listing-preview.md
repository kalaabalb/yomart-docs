## Full description

Explore the YoMart catalogue of smartphones and everyday electronics.

Browse product collections, explore categories and brands, search the catalogue, view product details and images, save favourites, and manage your YoMart account.

This closed-test version is a catalogue preview. Product prices and availability are being confirmed. Adding products to a purchasable order, checkout and payment are intentionally unavailable in this test build. Do not transfer money or upload payment receipts as part of this preview.

We are testing the app experience across Android devices and networks. Please report inaccurate product information, missing images, sign-in problems, navigation issues, layout defects and accessibility concerns through the feedback channel supplied with your test invitation.
